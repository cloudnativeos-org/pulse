The layout for "How this was made": a fact rail and the method text.

**Provide:** dates, counts and states for the rail (collected, re-verified, profiles, corrections, deep dives, maintainer review), and the section text.

**Layout:** a 13rem rail on the left, sticky on screen, with `t-eyebrow` labels; the text on the right with numbered steps in serif. Under 760px the rail becomes a two-column grid above the text.

Counts in the rail are computed from the data at build time (corrections are summed from every profile's `corrections` list).

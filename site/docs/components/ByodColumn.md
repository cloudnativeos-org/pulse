The "Bring your own distro" column: can the entry run on a base distribution the reader chooses? It is the only distinction the matrix draws between entries; there are no group headers.

**Provide:** the draft field `base_choice` (`drafts/base_choice.yaml`, not yet in the dataset): `adopter-chosen` renders Yes (Documented), `own-base-only` renders "No, ships its own base" (Inferred, with the inference in the tooltip), `unknown` renders Unknown. Every cell cites its source.

**Treatment**
- The whole column sits on its own tinted ground between two `rule-strong` verticals. It stands out by structure as well as tone.
- Yes values are semibold with a `▸` marker and a detail line (how many bases, which have released images).

**States:** Yes (Documented), No (Inferred), Unknown, and for a pending entry, nothing.

If you work on one of the projects in the Pulse, I would like your help. Every project gets the same request: check that your project is represented correctly and fairly. It usually takes about fifteen minutes, and saying no is fine.

That is the whole request. It is not a request to endorse the Pulse, to agree with how it is organised, or to review any other project. Correcting a fact about your project does not mean you agree with anything else here, and I will never present it as agreement.

What to check

  • Your project's data sheet: click its name in the Matrix. It lists every value from the matrix in full, with its evidence and sources.
  • Your row in the Comparison matrix. Each cell shows a short value with its evidence label. Your data sheet has the full text behind every cell.
  • Your project's profile in the repository. Every claim carries an evidence label and a source, so you can see where each one came from.
  • The open questions in your profile. These are the things I could not settle from public sources, and they are where your help counts most.

How to send a correction

While the Pulse is in preview, the repository is private. Send me your GitHub username and I will give you access, or just send me the correction with a source, through the contact links on mauromorales.com. With access, you can open an issue with the correction form or send a pull request against your project's profile with a primary source for the correct value. Every correction is recorded in the profile with its date and its source, so the history stays visible in the data. If you disagree with a claim the sources support, I record your view next to it rather than hide it. See Found a mistake, or something missing? for the links.

The same request goes to every project in the Pulse, including Kairos and Hadron. I am one of the maintainers of both, and I work for Spectro Cloud, so I am asking another Kairos maintainer to review those two instead of doing it myself.

Beyond a review

If you like the format and the method, I would welcome turning the Pulse into a group effort: a comparison kept up by people from several of these projects, not by me alone. Tell me if that interests you.

# Code of conduct

The cloud native OS Pulse adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the Pulse: in
the repository, in issues and pull requests, and in any other space that the
Pulse runs. The Pulse does not write its own code of conduct and does not
change the text of the covenant. Version 2.1 is the version in force. Changing
the version follows [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Enforcement contact

The enforcement contact is Mauro Morales
([@mauromorales](https://github.com/mauromorales)), for now. Report a breach to
him through the contact links on [mauromorales.com](https://mauromorales.com/).

An outside person will be named through a public issue that stays open for 14
days, before the bootstrap in GOVERNANCE.md ends. That person is listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). None is named yet. Until then, a report
about Mauro has no independent contact. GOVERNANCE.md lists this under Known
limits.

## Removing a maintainer

No finding by the enforcement contact alone removes a maintainer. A removal for
a breach follows [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), section Removing a maintainer.
It needs a written finding from the outside person. Until an outside person is
named, nobody is removed for a breach.
